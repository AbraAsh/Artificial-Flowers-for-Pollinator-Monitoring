# Artificial Flowers for Pollinator Monitoring

3D-printable artificial flower designs developed for automated, camera-based
pollinator monitoring, as part of the PhD ***Designing Artificial Flowers for Automated Pollinator Monitoring***
at Cranfield University. New flower models and improved versions of the current designs will be added in future releases. 

## Files

Designs are grouped by the study they were used in.

### `Chapter_3/`
**Testing the Efficacy of Artificial Flowers as a Novel Attractant for Automated Pollinator Monitoring**:

This chapter tested one artificial flower design against traditional monitoring attractants, pan traps and coloured paper, to establish baseline effectiveness relative to existing methods. This chapter has been peer-reviewed and published as a paper:

Ash, A., Hallett, S., Carvell, C., Williams, L., & August, T. (2026). Testing the efficacy of artificial flowers as a novel attractant for automated pollinator monitoring. Journal of Pollination Ecology, 41, 137–153. https://doi.org/10.26786/1920-7603(2026)893

Radially symmetrical, five-petalled, bowl-shaped flower with a stem, based on a
buttercup (*Ranunculus* spp.). Painted UV-fluorescent yellow, white, and blue
with a UV-nonreactive green stem.

### `Chapter_4/`
**Multimodal Attraction Cues in Artificial Flowers Affect Insect Pollinator Visits**

This chapter tested the effect of multimodal attraction cues in a 2³ factorial of scent, radiating line nectar guides, and a yellow/UV non-reactive centre on insect attraction to a generalist flower shape. 

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `6_petal_RS_flower.stl` | Radially symmetrical, six-petaled, bowl-shaped flower with a nectary hole in the centre. Painted UV-fluorescent yellow, white, blue, and purple with a UV-nonreactive yellow centre, and broken line, radiating nectar guides (originally painted in this chapter with thin Sharpie but would recommend paint instead)|

### `Chapter_5/`
**Evaluating Specialist Artificial Flowers for Targeted Bee and Butterfly Monitoring**

This chapter tested group-specific artificial flowers against a generalist baseline across two independent settings: a wildflower meadow field site (bee-targeted) and a butterfly house (butterfly-targeted).

#### `Generalist/`
Continues the progression of design from Chapters 3 and 4. 5-petaled, radially symmetrical flower with a nectary hole in the centre. 

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `Generalist.stl` | 5-petaled, radially symmetrical flower with a nectary hole in the centre. Painted UV-fluorescent yellow, white, and blue with a UV-nonreactive yellow centre, and brown broken, radiating nectar guides |

#### `Bee_Specialist/`
Designed around bee preferences for blue, violet and yellow colouration, nectar guides,
and tubular or bilaterally symmetrical forms.

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `bee_Black_Eyed_Susan.stl` | Based on *Rudbeckia hirta*. Radially symmetrical with thirteen ray petals and an approximated composite head. Painted UV-fluorescent yellow with a dark brown centre. |
| `bee_Borage.stl` | Based on *Borago officinalis*. Five-petalled, radially symmetrical, with bracts around the nectary. Painted UV-fluorescent blue with white bracts that have brown tips. Anthers omitted to leave room for the wick. |
| `bee_Lavender.stl` | Based on *Lavandula* spp. Small, tubular, bilaterally symmetrical flower with two upper and three lower petals. Painted violet. |
| `bee_Purple_Flower.stl` | Not based on a single species. A larger bilaterally symmetrical flower combining bee-associated traits. Painted violet with white nectar guides and a yellow ring around the nectary. |

#### `Butterfly_Specialist/`
Designed around butterfly preferences for flat landing platforms, tubular
florets, and red, orange, yellow, and violet colouration. All painted with
non-UV-fluorescent paints.

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `butterfly_Buddleia.stl` | Based on *Buddleja davidii*. Four-petalled, radially symmetrical tubular flower with textured petal edges. Painted violet with a yellow nectary ring. |
| `butterfly_Hibiscus.stl` | Based on *Hibiscus* spp. Single large, upward-facing five-petalled flower with textured petals. Reproductive organs omitted to avoid camera depth-of-focus issues. Painted red. |

## Printing

All flowers were printed on Elegoo resin printers (Saturn 3 for Chapter 3,
Jupiter SE for Chapters 4 and 5) using ABS-like resin. Files were prepared in
ChituBox with automatic supports. After printing, flowers were washed in
isopropyl alcohol, UV-cured, and supports removed by hand.

A resin printer is recommended for fine features such as stamens. Printing can
also be outsourced to a print service or local makerspace.

Print settings will vary by printer and resin, so use the settings that work
best for your set-up.

## Painting and scent delivery

Flowers were primed white, spray-painted, detailed by hand, and sealed with a
varnish containing no UV blocker. Nectary holes are sized for cotton wicks
that deliver scented water from a reservoir beneath the flower. Full paint
products, scents, and set-up details are given in the relevant chapter or paper. 

Feel free to paint as you see fit. The colour schemes described in the file
tables are those tested in the studies, so use these if you want results
comparable to the published findings.
